Syarti Account Deletion Request

This page explains how users can request deletion of their Syarti account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Syarti account, send an email to:

your-email@example.com

Please include the email address associated with your Syarti account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete:

  • Your account information (name and email address).
  • Your saved vehicle information.
  • Maintenance records.
  • Expense records.
  • Other data associated with your account.

Data Retention

Some information may be retained if required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Deleted data will not be used for providing app services.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are usually processed within a reasonable period after verification of ownership.

Contact

For any questions regarding account deletion, contact us at:

your-email@example.com
