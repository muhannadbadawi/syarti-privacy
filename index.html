Syarti Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 14, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Syarti. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Syarti mobile application.

Information We Collect

When you use Syarti, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication information

Vehicle Information

  • Vehicle make and model
  • Vehicle year
  • License plate information (if provided)
  • Vehicle mileage
  • Maintenance records
  • Service history
  • Fuel expenses
  • Insurance information (if provided)
  • Other vehicle-related expenses entered by the user

Device Information

We may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Crash and diagnostic information

How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store and synchronize your vehicle information.
  • Track maintenance history and expenses.
  • Send maintenance reminders.
  • Improve application performance.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain application security.

Data Storage

Your information is securely stored using Google Firebase services. We use reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Notifications

Syarti may send notifications related to vehicle maintenance, including oil changes, tires, insurance renewal, and other reminders.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may use trusted third-party services such as Google Firebase to provide application functionality.

Data Retention

Your information is stored while your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data.

Children's Privacy

Syarti is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information.

Security

We use appropriate security practices to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
